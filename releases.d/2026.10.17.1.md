## Face ID — unlock with your face

Pulsar OS can now recognize you with the webcam. It works at the **login
screen**, on the **lock screen**, and to approve system actions — powered by
the Gaze face authentication engine.

### How to set it up

1. Open **Settings → Privacy → Biometrics & Presence** (or launch
   **Pulsar Biometrics** from the app grid).
2. Turn on the **Face ID** switch and press **Set Up Face ID…**.
3. Sit in front of the camera and follow the prompts to enroll your face,
   slowly turning your head so it learns you from different angles.
4. Done! From now on you just look at the camera to unlock.

### What do you need?

- Any webcam (USB or built-in).
- A processor with **AVX2** support (Intel since 2013, AMD since 2015).
  On older CPUs the system detects it and politely leaves Face ID disabled.

### Presence auto-lock

Same panel: enable it and Pulsar OS **locks the session when you walk away**
and stays unlocked while it recognizes you. You can adjust how long it waits,
and there is a **Live Presence & Face ID Diagnostics** window to test the
camera and the recognition engine in real time.

## Touch ID — fingerprint unlock

- Enroll your fingerprints in the same **Biometrics & Presence** panel with
  the graphical assistant (**Manage Fingerprints…**).
- Works with any fingerprint reader supported by Linux (most built-in
  laptop readers).
- Use it to unlock the screen and to approve system actions **without
  typing your password**.

## Login screen

The login screen now shows a **biometrics indicator**: you can sign in with
your face or your fingerprint right at login, before the desktop loads.

## New wallpapers

A fresh wallpaper collection ships with this update: official **Pulsar OS
backgrounds** plus a full set of classic macOS-style wallpapers (Big Sur,
Sequoia, Sonoma, Tahoe Beach, Golden Gate and more), with light and dark
variants integrated in **Settings → Appearance**.

## Sayri

The voice assistant got a major update: a new **agents and gateway** command
line tools, a **Discord memory gateway** so Sayri can remember conversations,
a modernized **Cajita** settings window, and smarter Clippy positioning on
multi-monitor setups.

## Rounded corners & blur (GNOME 51)

Fixed the annoying **corner spikes** in blurred areas on GNOME 51: blur now
clips cleanly to the rounded corners of windows, popup menus, the
notification center and the notch shadow — no more square blur sticking out
of rounded corners.

## Stability improvements

- **Notification Center**: big stability overhaul — no more double-blurred
  OSD and a fixed crash race when logging out.
- Control Center and Notification Center are now blurred correctly in
  normal (non-glass) mode too.
- Fixed a freeze loop in the Activities overview and apps getting stuck.
- Hardened the macOS Control Centre against crashes.
- Fixed an error flood caused by liquid-glass dialogs.
- General GNOME 51 extension compatibility pass.
